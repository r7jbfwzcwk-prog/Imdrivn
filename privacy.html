Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

IM DRIVN is a car marketplace and community operated by Aus Auto Haus Pty Ltd (ABN 18 692 096 727), trading as IM DRIVN, in Australia. This policy is written for the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). It describes the personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can access, correct, or delete it.

This page is the privacy policy for the IM DRIVN website at imdrivn.com.au, including sign-in with Google and sign-in with Apple.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run an account, a listing, a message, or a paid feature.

Account details

When you create an account we collect your email address and the @handle you choose. If you add a phone number, we collect that too. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the basic profile those services send, described below.

Google user data: name, email, and profile picture

If you choose Continue with Google, IM DRIVN requests and receives only these fields from your Google account:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your profile picture

We do not request your Google contacts, your Google Drive files, your Gmail contents, your calendar, your location history, or any other Google data.

Apple sign-in

If you choose Continue with Apple, we receive the name, email address, and profile information Apple provides. Apple may hide your real email behind a private-relay address (a @privaterelay.appleid.com address). Apple may send your name only the first time you sign in, or not at all. A missing name does not block your account. You still choose your own @handle.

Listings, photos, messages, and forum posts

If you list a car we collect the details you enter (make, model, year, price, kilometres, location, description, and similar fields) and the photos you upload. If you message another member, we collect the message text and the time it was sent. If you post on the forum, we collect the post or reply and the time it was published.

Payments

Paid features such as extra boosts are charged by Stripe. Stripe collects your card number and other payment details and stores them under Stripe's own terms. IM DRIVN does not store your full card number. We keep a record that a payment happened: the amount, the currency, the status, and a Stripe reference, so we can apply the feature you paid for and keep our accounts.

VIN and PPSR searches

If you order a PPSR VIN report we collect the VIN you type and the email address the report should be sent to, plus the fact that the search was ordered and supplied.

How we use personal information

We use personal information to:

  • create your account and sign you in
  • show your @handle, name, and profile picture on the account
  • publish the listings and photos you choose to post
  • deliver messages between members and show forum posts
  • send email you asked for, including account mail and PPSR reports
  • take payment for a paid feature and record that the payment succeeded
  • investigate reports, scams, and breaches of the terms
  • meet tax, accounting, and other legal duties

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for third-party advertising. We do not use it to train generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

How we use Google user data

Google user data means the name, email address, and profile picture we receive when you use Continue with Google.

We use that Google user data solely to create your IM DRIVN account and to sign you in on later visits. The name and profile picture identify the account. The email address is the address we use to recognise the account and to send account email. We do not use Google user data for advertising, for marketing to other people, for selling data, or for credit, insurance, or employment decisions.

We do not let humans read your Google user data except where you ask us to (for example a support email), where we need to investigate a security incident or a breach of the terms, or where the law requires it.

IM DRIVN's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with the service providers who process it on our instructions so IM DRIVN can work, and when the law requires us to.

  • Supabase hosts the database, authentication, and the photos and files you upload. Your account record, listings, messages, and forum posts are stored there.
  • Stripe processes payments. Stripe receives the payment details you enter on Stripe's checkout, and we receive the payment result.
  • Resend sends transactional email, such as account messages and mail from the contact form.
  • Google is used only if you choose Continue with Google, and only for the name, email address, and profile picture described above.
  • Apple is used only if you choose Continue with Apple, and only for the profile Apple returns.

Other members see what you choose to publish: your @handle, your listings, your photos, and your forum posts. Private messages are shown to the people in that conversation. We do not publish your email address on a listing.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia. Where they do, we take reasonable steps so the handling stays consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles.

How long we keep it

We keep your account, listings, messages, and forum posts while the account is open and for as long as we need them to run IM DRIVN, deal with a dispute, or meet a legal duty.

Google user data (name, email address, and profile picture) is kept for the life of the account, because it is the sign-in identity. If you ask us to delete the account, we delete that Google user data from the IM DRIVN account, except for a record we are required to keep (for example a tax invoice).

Payment records are kept for the period Australian tax and accounting law requires, including after the account is closed. VIN search requests are kept so we can show that the report was supplied. When we no longer need information, we delete it or de-identify it.

Deletion

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information attached to it, including Google user data we stored (your name, email address, and profile picture from Google). Email ohhey@imdrivn.com.au from the email address on the account and write that you want the account deleted.

We will confirm it is your account and delete the sign-in, the Google or Apple profile fields we stored, and the account record. Listings and photos you uploaded are removed with the account unless a legal duty says we must keep a copy. A message already delivered to another member may remain in that member's inbox, with your @handle removed where we can do that without breaking their copy of the conversation. Payment records that tax law requires us to keep are retained for that period and then deleted.

You can also revoke IM DRIVN's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops future sign-in with Google. To delete data we already stored, use the email above.

Access and correction

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, and you can ask us to correct it if it is wrong. Email ohhey@imdrivn.com.au. We respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm it is you. If we refuse a request where the law allows us to, we will tell you why, unless the law says not to.

Cookies and browser storage

We use cookies and similar storage, including local storage in your browser, to keep you signed in, to remember a preference such as the last lane you viewed, and to protect the sign-in flow. We do not use cookies to sell advertising. If you clear cookies and site data you will need to sign in again.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your information wrongly, email ohhey@imdrivn.com.au and we will look into it. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au or by phone on 1300 363 992.

Contact

Privacy questions, access requests, correction requests, and deletion requests: ohhey@imdrivn.com.au
