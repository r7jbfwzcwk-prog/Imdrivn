Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

IM DRIVN is operated by Aus Auto Haus Pty Ltd (ABN 18 692 096 727), trading as IM DRIVN, in Australia. This policy explains how we handle personal information under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What we collect

We collect personal information you give us, and information created when you use the site.

  • Account details. Your email address, the @handle you choose, and a phone number if you add one. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive the basic profile those services send: your name, email address, and profile picture. Apple may hide your email behind a private-relay address, and Apple may only send your name the first time you sign in.
  • Listings and photos. Vehicle details, prices, descriptions, locations, and the photos you upload.
  • Messages and forum posts. The messages you send to other members, and the posts and replies you write on the forum.
  • Payments. Boosts, membership, and other paid features are charged by Stripe. Stripe collects and stores your card and payment details. We receive a record of the payment (such as the amount, status, and a Stripe customer reference). We do not store your full card number.
  • VIN and PPSR searches. If you order a PPSR VIN report, we collect the VIN you enter and the email address the report is sent to, plus the fact that a search was ordered.

How we use it

We use personal information to create and run your account, show your listings, deliver messages and forum posts, send the emails you ask for (including PPSR reports, account messages, and replies to contact forms), take payment for paid features, keep the site safe, and meet our legal duties.

We do not sell personal information.

Google user data

If you choose Continue with Google, IM DRIVN requests only your basic Google profile: your name, email address, and profile picture. We use that information solely to create your IM DRIVN account and to sign you in. We do not use Google user data for advertising. We do not sell it. We do not share it with anyone except the service providers who process it on our instructions so the account can work, and we do not use it to train generalised artificial-intelligence models.

IM DRIVN's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Apple sign-in

If you choose Continue with Apple, we receive the name, email (which may be a private-relay address), and profile information Apple provides, and we use it only to create your account and sign you in. A missing name does not stop you from using IM DRIVN. You still choose your own @handle.

Who else handles it

We use these providers to run the service. They process personal information only so we can provide IM DRIVN:

  • Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, and stored photos and files.
  • Stripe takes payments. Stripe's handling of card details is covered by Stripe.
  • Resend sends transactional email, such as account messages and contact-form mail.
  • Google signs you in if you choose Google, and only to the extent described above.
  • Apple signs you in if you choose Apple.

Some of these providers store data outside Australia. Where that happens, we take reasonable steps to see that your information is handled in line with the APPs.

Cookies and similar storage

We use cookies and browser storage (including local storage) to keep you signed in, remember preferences such as the lane you last viewed, and protect the sign-in flow. We do not use that storage to sell ads. You can clear cookies and site data in your browser. If you do, you will need to sign in again.

How long we keep it

We keep account, listing, message, and forum information while your account is open and for as long as we need it to run IM DRIVN, resolve disputes, and meet legal duties. Payment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, even if you close your account. VIN search requests are kept so we can show that the search was supplied. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or de-identify it.

Access, correction, and deletion

You can ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Email ohhey@imdrivn.com.au from the address on the account and tell us what you want. We will respond within a reasonable time. We may need to confirm it is you. We may refuse a request where the law says we can, and we will tell you why unless the law says not to.

Deleting an account removes your sign-in. Some material you posted (for example a message another member still needs, or a record we must keep) may remain where the law or the running of the service requires it. We will remove or de-identify it when we no longer need it.

Complaints

If you think we have handled your information wrongly, email ohhey@imdrivn.com.au and we will look into it. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Contact

Privacy questions and requests: ohhey@imdrivn.com.au
